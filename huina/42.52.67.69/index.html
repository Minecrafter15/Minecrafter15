FTL — Full Truckload Transportation

Ideal for large loads from 15,000 to 45,000 kilograms

Service Description

FTL transportation provides a dedicated truck exclusively for your cargo. The trailer travels directly from pickup to delivery without intermediate stops, minimizing handling risk and transit time. Ideal for time-sensitive or high-value shipments.

Advantages:

  • Direct delivery without reloading
  • Lower risk of damage
  • Fixed price per shipment
  • Faster transit times

Service Details

Load capacity:up to 45,000 kg
Volume:up to 70 m³
Trailer length:13.8 m / 15 m
Routes:Germany, France, Poland & more
Delivery time:1–5 business days
Tracking:GPS real-time
